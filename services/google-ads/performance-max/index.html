PMax, done properly

Feed the machine right,
or it feeds on you.

Performance Max spreads your budget across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Discover and Maps, all steered by machine learning. When it's fed clean conversion data, strong creative and honest guardrails, it finds buyers no manual campaign would reach. When it's fed junk, it optimises for junk, cheerfully spending your budget on clicks that were never going to become jobs, then reporting it as success.

Our PMax management is mostly about the inputs: conversion tracking that measures real enquiries instead of pageviews, asset groups built like proper ad campaigns, audience signals that give the machine a head start, and the exclusions that keep it honest. Then we watch what it's actually doing and correct it every week.

What's included

✓Conversion data hygiene: the machine optimises to what you count, so we make sure you're counting booked jobs.
✓Asset group builds: themed creative sets with strong headlines, images and video, per product or service line.
✓Audience signals: your customer lists, converters and best segments pointing the machine in the right direction.
✓Brand and junk exclusions: brand traffic split out honestly, spammy placements and irrelevant queries fenced off.
✓Search term monitoring: PMax finally shows its search categories, and we read them weekly.
✓Creative refresh cycles: assets rotated before fatigue sets in, judged on the machine's own strength ratings.
How we run it

The Performance Max playbook

the boring work that wins
1

Get the data right

PMax amplifies whatever your conversion tracking says success is. Before launch we make sure it says booked jobs, not button clicks, or the machine will happily optimise your budget into a hole.

2

Build real asset groups

One lazy asset group with your logo and a stock photo is not a campaign. We build themed groups per service or product line, each with creative strong enough to compete everywhere PMax serves.

3

Signal, then supervise

Audience signals and search themes give the algorithm a running start; weekly reviews of placements, categories and spend distribution keep it accountable.

4

Protect the brand line

Brand traffic gets excluded or measured separately, so PMax earns its keep on genuinely new demand instead of taking credit for people who already knew you.

Performance Max management with adult supervision

The industry has a love-hate relationship with Performance Max for one reason: it does exactly what it's told, and most accounts tell it the wrong things. Feed it soft conversions and it hunts soft conversions. Skip the brand exclusions and it buys your own customers back and calls it growth. Give it one thin asset group and it serves weak creative across a million placements. Our PMax work is the un-glamorous discipline that turns the machine into an asset: clean conversion definitions, proper creative volume, deliberate audience signals, and weekly interrogation of where the money actually went.

Run that way, PMax is genuinely brilliant, especially paired with Search for the high-intent core and Shopping for product demand. Run lazily, it's the most expensive set-and-forget in advertising. If you're not sure which version you've got, that's precisely what the free audit is for.

What to expect: honesty about the learning phase. PMax needs one to two weeks to calibrate and shouldn't be judged before week four, which is exactly why we set it up right the first time rather than restarting it monthly. From there, expect steady incremental volume on top of your Search core, with weekly spend-distribution reads keeping the machine accountable.

Ask us anything
What is Performance Max, in plain English?+
One campaign that serves across all of Google, Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Discover and Maps, with machine learning deciding where your budget goes based on the conversion data and creative you feed it. Brilliant inputs, brilliant results; lazy inputs, expensive lessons.
Should I run Performance Max or Search campaigns?+
Usually both. Search gives you precision on your proven money keywords; PMax hunts incremental demand across everything else. The structure matters so they don't cannibalise each other, which is exactly the kind of architecture we build.
Why did my Performance Max campaign spend so much with nothing to show?+
Nine times out of ten: conversion tracking counting the wrong things, no brand exclusions, or asset groups too thin to compete. PMax doesn't fail loudly, it optimises confidently toward whatever it was told, which is why the telling is the job.
What creative do I need for PMax?+
Headlines, descriptions, images in multiple ratios, your logo, and ideally short video. Don't have video? Google will auto-generate one, and you don't want that representing your brand, so we build proper asset sets instead.
Can Performance Max work for service businesses, not just ecommerce?+
Absolutely, lead-gen PMax is half of what we run. The non-negotiables are stricter though: lead quality tracking (not just form fills) and tight exclusions, because the machine will find you infinite junk leads if junk is what gets counted.
Why choose Nexevo Digitals for Performance Max?+
Because we treat PMax like a powerful tool that needs supervision rather than a magic button. Clean data in, real creative in, weekly accountability on where the spend went, and honest reporting on what it earned. Your account, month to month, audit's free.
