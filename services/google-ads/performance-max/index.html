Why it matters

Performance Max management with adult supervision

The industry has a love-hate relationship with Performance Max for one reason: it does exactly what it's told, and most accounts tell it the wrong things. Feed it soft conversions and it hunts soft conversions. Skip the brand exclusions and it buys your own customers back and calls it growth. Give it one thin asset group and it serves weak creative across a million placements. Our PMax work is the un-glamorous discipline that turns the machine into an asset: clean conversion definitions, proper creative volume, deliberate audience signals, and weekly interrogation of where the money actually went.

Run that way, PMax is genuinely brilliant, especially paired with Search for the high-intent core and Shopping for product demand. Run lazily, it's the most expensive set-and-forget in advertising. If you're not sure which version you've got, that's precisely what the free audit is for.

What to expect: honesty about the learning phase. PMax needs one to two weeks to calibrate and shouldn't be judged before week four, which is exactly why we set it up right the first time rather than restarting it monthly. From there, expect steady incremental volume on top of your Search core, with weekly spend-distribution reads keeping the machine accountable.

What is Performance Max, in plain English?+
One campaign that serves across all of Google, Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Discover and Maps, with machine learning deciding where your budget goes based on the conversion data and creative you feed it. Brilliant inputs, brilliant results; lazy inputs, expensive lessons.
Should I run Performance Max or Search campaigns?+
Usually both. Search gives you precision on your proven money keywords; PMax hunts incremental demand across everything else. The structure matters so they don't cannibalise each other, which is exactly the kind of architecture we build.
Why did my Performance Max campaign spend so much with nothing to show?+
Nine times out of ten: conversion tracking counting the wrong things, no brand exclusions, or asset groups too thin to compete. PMax doesn't fail loudly, it optimizes confidently toward whatever it was told, which is why the telling is the job.
What creative do I need for PMax?+
Headlines, descriptions, images in multiple ratios, your logo, and ideally short video. Don't have video? Google will auto-generate one, and you don't want that representing your brand, so we build proper asset sets instead.
Can Performance Max work for service businesses, not just ecommerce?+
Absolutely, lead-gen PMax is half of what we run. The non-negotiables are stricter though: lead quality tracking (not just form fills) and tight exclusions, because the machine will find you infinite junk leads if junk is what gets counted.
