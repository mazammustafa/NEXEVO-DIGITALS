How long does logo design take?+
Most logos take two to three weeks from discovery to final files, depending on how quickly feedback comes back.
How many concepts and revisions do I get?+
We present a set of initial concepts, then refine your favorite through rounds of revisions. The exact number is set out in your quote, so there are no surprises.
Do I own the logo?+
Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the final logo and all its files are yours to use however you like.
What files will I receive?+
Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG and PDF) for print and signage, plus PNG and JPG for web and social, in full color, black and white versions.
Can you refresh my existing logo?+
Yes. If your current logo has recognition worth keeping, we can modernize it rather than starting from scratch.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for logo design?+
Because we design logos that work in the real world of marketing: crisp on a phone screen, clear in a Google listing and bold on a work vehicle.
