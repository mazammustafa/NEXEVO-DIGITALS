What does a website care plan include?+
Managed updates, daily backups, security monitoring and response, uptime and form checks, speed maintenance and a monthly allowance for content changes. Essentially: everything a healthy site needs that owners never have time for.
My host says they do backups, isn't that enough?+
Host backups are a safety net with holes: often infrequent, stored on the same infrastructure and slow to restore. Ours are daily, off-site and verified, and we have restored enough sites to know the difference matters.
What happens if my site gets hacked?+
We fix it, included in the plan: malware removed, the entry point closed, Google warnings cleared and the site restored. Response, not just monitoring, is the point of paying for care.
Can I send through small changes?+
Yes, that is half the value: pricing updates, new photos, staff changes, seasonal notices, email them through and they are handled inside your monthly window. No quotes for five-minute jobs.
Do you care for sites you didn't build?+
Yes, after a health check so we know what we are inheriting. Most sites come to us from exactly that situation: built by someone, maintained by no one.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for website care?+
Because the site we maintain is usually a site we are actively growing with ads and SEO, so care is not passive insurance: it is performance protection, with a team that notices problems because we are in your numbers weekly.
