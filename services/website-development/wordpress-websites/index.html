How much does a WordPress website cost?+
Depends on size and function: a lean service-business site is a different project to a large content platform. We quote fixed prices after a short conversation about goals, and the quote is the price, no scope-creep ambushes.
How long does a build take?+
Most business sites go live in four to eight weeks depending on size and how fast content decisions come back. We agree the timeline upfront and keep you inside the loop the whole way.
Will I be able to edit it myself?+
Yes, non-negotiable for us: your own logins, a usable editor and a handover walkthrough. Plenty of clients have us keep maintaining it anyway, but that is a choice, not a hostage situation.
Do you write the content or do I?+
We write it, with your input. Copy that converts is half the build, so it is included. You know your business; we know what makes visitors inquire; the pages get both.
Can you rebuild or improve my existing site instead?+
Often, yes. If the bones are good, targeted improvements beat a rebuild on cost and speed. The free review tells you honestly which situation you are in.
