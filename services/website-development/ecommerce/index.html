Do you build on Shopify or WooCommerce?+
Our default is WooCommerce for ownership, flexibility and SEO strength, but we work with what fits your operation. The recommendation comes after understanding your products, volumes and workflows, not before.
How much does an e-commerce site cost?+
It scales with catalog size and functionality: a focused store is a very different build to a complex one with integrations. Fixed quote after a short discovery conversation, and the quote is the price.
Can you migrate my existing store?+
Yes, products, customers, orders and, critically, SEO: URL mapping and redirects handled so rankings survive the move. Migrations fail on the details, so we sweat them.
Will the store be ready for Google Shopping ads?+
From day one: clean product feeds, proper attributes, purchase tracking and Merchant Center readiness are part of the build, not an afterthought. Most of our stores start running Shopping campaigns at launch.
What about ongoing changes and products?+
You get full editing control and a walkthrough, add products, change prices, run sales yourself. For businesses that would rather not, our website care plans handle it.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for e-commerce?+
Because we build the store the way the marketing needs it: feed-ready, trackable, fast and structured to rank. The team that builds it is the team that grows it, so nothing gets built twice.
