What is conversion rate optimization?+
The systematic practice of increasing the percentage of visitors who inquire or buy: diagnosing where they drop off, testing changes against real behavior, and keeping what measurably works. Same traffic, more outcomes.
How much improvement is realistic?+
Sites that have never been optimized commonly gain 30 to 100 percent on conversion rate across a few months of testing; mature sites grind out smaller compounding wins. We benchmark yours honestly in the audit before promising anything.
Do I need lots of traffic for CRO?+
Formal A/B testing needs volume, but lower-traffic sites still benefit hugely from evidence-based fixes: recordings and heatmaps reveal friction at any scale. We match the method to your numbers.
What gets tested first?+
The biggest suspected leaks: usually the core offer's clarity, the form's length, proof placement and mobile experience. Button colors come last, despite their reputation.
How does CRO interact with my ads?+
Directly: every point of conversion rate cuts your cost per lead across every channel at once. It is why we treat CRO as part of campaign management rather than a separate discipline.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for CRO?+
Because we optimize against real revenue data from accounts we run daily, not generic best-practice lists. Evidence first, tests measured properly, and wins that compound permanently into everything else you do.
