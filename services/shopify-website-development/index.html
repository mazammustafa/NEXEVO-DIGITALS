Should I use Shopify or WooCommerce?+
Shopify is a great fit if you want a reliable, low-maintenance store that's quick to manage. WooCommerce suits businesses that need more control or already run on WordPress. We'll recommend the right one after understanding your products and workflow.
How much does a Shopify website cost?+
It depends on the number of products, the design and any custom features. You get a fixed quote after a short discovery conversation, and the quote is the price.
Can you move my existing store to Shopify?+
Yes. We migrate products, customers and order history, and set up redirects so your Google rankings survive the move.
I already have a Shopify store. Can you improve it?+
Absolutely. We can redesign your theme, speed it up, fix product pages or add features. We'll start with a free review and show you where sales are slipping.
Will I be able to manage the store myself?+
Yes. Shopify is easy to run day to day, and we walk you through adding products, changing prices, running sales and handling orders.
