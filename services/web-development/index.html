What is the difference between web design and web development?+
Design is how your site looks and guides people to inquire; development is how it's built and how it works: speed, functionality, integrations and security. We do both in-house, so nothing gets lost between the two.
What platforms do you develop on?+
Mostly WordPress for business websites and Shopify or WooCommerce for online stores, with custom code where a project needs it. We recommend the platform after understanding your goals, not before.
How long does a web development project take?+
A typical business website takes four to eight weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger builds with custom features or integrations take longer, and you get a clear timeline before anything starts.
Can you work on my existing website?+
Yes. We can fix, speed up or extend an existing site. We'll start with an audit and tell you honestly whether it's worth improving or better rebuilt.
Will I be able to update the site myself?+
Yes. You get full editing access and a walkthrough, so changing text, images and pages is easy. If you'd rather not, we can handle updates for you.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for web development?+
Because we build websites that the marketing can actually use: fast, trackable and structured to rank. The team that builds it is the team that grows it, so nothing needs rebuilding later.
