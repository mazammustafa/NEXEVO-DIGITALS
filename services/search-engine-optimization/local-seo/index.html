What is local SEO exactly?+
It is the work that gets you found for searches with local intent: the map pack, 'near me' searches, and service-plus-neighborhood queries. It combines your Google Business Profile, reviews, citations and location-relevant site content into one system.
How is local SEO different from normal SEO?+
Normal SEO fights for the organic list; local SEO also fights for the map pack, which runs on different signals: proximity, profile quality, review patterns and local relevance. Service businesses usually need both, and the map pack often returns faster.
How long until I show in the map pack?+
Uncompetitive neighborhoods can move within weeks; contested metro areas typically take three to six months of consistent signals. Profile fixes and review velocity are usually the fastest levers, so early movement is common.
Do reviews really affect rankings?+
Heavily. Volume, recency, rating and even the words reviewers use all feed the map pack algorithm, and they double as conversion proof. That is why we systemize the ask instead of leaving reviews to chance.
Can you do local SEO for multiple locations?+
Yes. Multi-location businesses get a profile per location, location pages that are genuinely distinct, and structure that stops the locations competing with each other. It is one of our most common builds.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for local SEO?+
Because we measure it like a revenue channel: profile calls, direction requests and form fills, tracked and reported monthly. Month to month, no lock-ins, and the free audit shows you exactly what is holding your map rankings back.
