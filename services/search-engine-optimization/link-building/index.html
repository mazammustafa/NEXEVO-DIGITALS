Do links still matter for SEO?+
Enormously. Content and technical health get you in the game; links decide who wins the contested searches. Google has confirmed repeatedly that links remain among its strongest signals.
What makes a link good or bad?+
Good: relevant site, real audience, editorial placement, natural anchor. Bad: link farms, irrelevant directories, paid networks and anything a competitor could report. One good link outweighs fifty bad ones, and bad ones carry risk.
How many links do I need?+
Depends entirely on your market: we benchmark the sites actually ranking for your targets and build to close the gap. Some niches need a handful of strong links; contested metro trades need sustained velocity.
Is link building safe? I've heard about penalties.+
Bought-in-bulk links get penalised; earned links do not. Our work is white-hat by design: real sites, real relevance, natural patterns. We also clean up risky historical links as part of the audit.
How long until links move rankings?+
nexevovidual links take weeks to be crawled and weighed; profile-level impact typically shows over two to four months of consistent building. It compounds, which is exactly why it is worth doing properly.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for link building?+
Full transparency, every link reported with its source, American relevance over offshore junk, and quality thresholds we will not lower to hit a number. Your authority is an asset; we build it like one.
