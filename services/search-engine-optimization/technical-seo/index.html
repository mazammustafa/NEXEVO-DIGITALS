What does technical SEO actually cover?+
Everything that affects how search engines access and understand your site: speed, crawling, indexing, redirects, canonical tags, schema, mobile rendering and architecture. It is the foundation layer that content and links build on.
How do I know if my site has technical problems?+
Common tells: pages missing from Google, rankings that plateau despite good content, slow mobile loads, or Search Console warnings nobody has looked at. The free audit surfaces all of it in plain English.
Will fixing technical issues improve rankings on its own?+
Often, yes, especially when crawl or indexing problems were suppressing pages. On healthier sites it is a multiplier: the same content and links simply work harder once the foundations are solid.
What are Core Web Vitals and do they matter?+
Google's measurements of loading speed, interactivity and visual stability. They are a confirmed ranking input and a huge conversion factor, slow sites lose people before the page finishes painting.
Is technical SEO a one-off project or ongoing?+
Both. The initial repair is a project; after that, sites drift, plugins update, content teams add pages, so ongoing monitoring catches regressions before they cost rankings. Our packages include it.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for technical SEO?+
Because we build and rank websites ourselves, so the fixes come from people who live in the consequences. Prioritized by impact, documented in plain English, and measured in rankings and inquiries.
