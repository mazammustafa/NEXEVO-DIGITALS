Which platforms do you manage?+
Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok. We recommend the ones your customers actually use, rather than spreading your brand thin across all of them.
How many posts will I get each month?+
It depends on your plan and goals. We agree the posting schedule upfront, and you always know exactly what is going out and when.
Do you create the content or do I?+
We do. We design the graphics, write the captions and edit video. If you can send us photos or clips from jobs, even better, because real content from your business performs best.
Do I get to approve posts before they go live?+
Yes. You see the content calendar in advance and can approve or request changes before anything is published.
Is paid advertising included?+
Social media management covers your organic content and community. Paid ad campaigns can be added on and are quoted separately.
Why choose Nexevo Digitals for social media?+
Because we treat social as part of your marketing, not a box to tick. Your posts, website and ads all tell the same story, so every channel works harder.
