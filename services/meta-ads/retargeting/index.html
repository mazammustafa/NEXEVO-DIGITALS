They looked.

Your warmest audience is the one you already paid for: the people who visited the site, watched the video, opened the form and drifted off. Retargeting reaches exactly them with the message that matches where they stopped, and it routinely produces the cheapest conversions in the whole account.

Done lazily it is the same ad following people around for a month, which burns goodwill. Done properly it is a sequence: proof for the hesitant, urgency for the close, an easy next step for everyone, frequency-capped so it persuades without stalking.

What is retargeting exactly?+
Showing ads specifically to people who have already interacted with you: visited the site, watched a video, opened a lead form or bought before. The Meta Pixel and Conversions API build the audiences; the campaigns bring them back.
Why is retargeting so cheap per lead?+
Because the hardest part, earning attention from a stranger, is already done. Warm audiences click more and convert more, so the algorithm charges less per result. It is consistently the best cost per lead in most accounts.
Isn't it creepy following people around?+
Lazy retargeting is: one ad, no cap, forever. We sequence messages, cap frequency and exclude converters immediately, so it feels like a helpful reminder rather than surveillance.
How big does my audience need to be?+
Meaningful retargeting starts around a few hundred people in a segment; below that, delivery gets patchy. If your traffic is thin we build the warm audience first with reach campaigns, then retarget it.
Can you retarget my Google Ads traffic on Meta?+
Yes, and it is one of the best plays in the book: search clicks that did not convert get followed up on social, where proof-style creative closes them. Running both channels under one roof makes it seamless.
Why choose Nexevo Digital for retargeting?+
Because we build the whole system: tracking wired properly, segments that mean something, creative for each stage and honest reporting on incremental value. Retargeting done right is quiet, cheap and relentless.
