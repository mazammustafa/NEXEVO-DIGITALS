Facebook Lead Ads,
straight answers.

What business owners ask us most. Anything else, bring it to your free audit.

Ask us anything
What are Facebook lead ads?+
Ads with a built-in instant form: the person taps, the form opens inside Facebook pre-filled with their details, they submit, and the lead arrives in your system. No website visit required, which slashes drop-off and cost per lead.
Are lead ad leads lower quality?+
Only when the form is lazy. Pre-filled forms attract everyone, so we add qualifying questions and design offers for buyers, which trades a little volume for a lot of quality. Speed of follow-up does the rest.
How fast do I get the leads?+
Instantly. We connect the forms to your email, phone or CRM so the lead lands the moment they submit, because a five-minute callback beats a next-day one by miles.
What does a Facebook lead cost?+
It varies wildly by industry and offer, from a few dollars for broad consumer services to more in competitive trades. The audit gives you honest benchmarks for your market before you spend anything.
Do lead ads work without a website?+
Yes, that is one of their superpowers: the whole journey happens inside Facebook. A strong page still helps for trust and retargeting, but lead ads can generate enquiries while a site is being built.
Why choose Nexevo Digitals for lead ads?+
Because we obsess over the unsexy parts that decide quality: qualifying questions, instant delivery, offer testing and follow-up. Leads are only valuable if they answer the phone and book, so that is the number we manage to.
