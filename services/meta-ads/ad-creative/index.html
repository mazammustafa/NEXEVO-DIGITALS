Why does creative matter more than targeting now?+
Because Meta's algorithm now finds audiences largely on its own, using engagement signals from the creative itself. A scroll-stopping ad literally teaches the system who to show it to, which makes creative the biggest lever you still control.
What is UGC and why does it work?+
User-generated-content style: real-person, direct-to-camera, phone-shot authenticity. It works because it reads as recommendation rather than advertisement, and feeds sit full of exactly that format. For service businesses it routinely beats polished production.
Do I need to appear in the ads myself?+
It helps, founders and staff convert brilliantly because they are believable, but it is not required. We work with creators, customers-as-testimonials and voiceover formats when you would rather stay behind the camera.
How often does creative need refreshing?+
In active accounts, winning ads typically fatigue within three to six weeks. We watch frequency and cost trends per ad and land replacements before the decay shows in your cost per lead.
What do you need from us to make creative?+
Access to the raw gold: job photos, before-and-afters, reviews, a few voice notes about common customer objections. We turn business-as-usual material into ads; a short monthly content session covers most accounts.
Why choose Nexevo Digitals for ad creative?+
Because our creative answers to the ad account, not a portfolio: everything we make is tested against cost per lead and iterated on data. Made in-house, refreshed on schedule, and never an optional extra.
