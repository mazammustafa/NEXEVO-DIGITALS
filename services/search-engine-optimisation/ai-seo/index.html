Ask us anything
What is AI SEO or GEO?+
Generative engine optimisation: making your business the source AI systems cite when they answer questions in your market, across Google's AI overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and the answer engines. Same goal as SEO, new referees.
Is traditional SEO dead then?+
No, and anyone selling that line is selling panic. AI systems draw from ranked, authoritative, structured content, which is what good SEO has always built. AI SEO is an extension, not a replacement.
How do you get cited by AI overviews?+
Direct answers high on the page, question-matched headings, clean schema, strong topical authority and a technically healthy site. The systems favour sources that answer clearly and can be trusted.
Can you track AI search visibility?+
Yes, imperfectly but usefully: we monitor AI overview appearances for target queries, assistant mentions and the referral traffic those surfaces send. The tooling is young, so we measure what is measurable and say so.
Should a local service business care about this?+
Increasingly, yes. 'Best plumber near me' asked to an assistant returns a shortlist, and being on it is the new map pack. Local entity signals and reviews feed those answers directly.
Why choose Nexevo Digitals for AI SEO?+
Because we treat it with evidence, not hype: measurable work, honest reporting on a fast-moving space, and every AI-focused improvement doubling as classic SEO gain. No snake oil, just structure and authority.
