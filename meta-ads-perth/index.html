Ask us anything
Are Meta Ads cheaper in Perth?+
Meaningfully. Audience costs typically run well under Sydney and Melbourne, so the same budget buys more attention and the pixel learns faster. Combined with WA's local loyalty, well-run Perth accounts post some of the best cost-per-lead numbers we see anywhere.
Does WA's local loyalty change the strategy?+
It shapes the creative above all. Perth audiences respond to visibly local businesses, real teams, real suburbs, WA phrasing, and reward them with conversion rates east-coast campaigns envy. Generic national-style creative is the most common money leak we fix here.
Which Perth areas can you target?+
Greater Perth end to end with suburb-level control, Joondalup to Mandurah, coast to hills, weighted by where your profitable jobs come from. The metro's shape makes geo-weighting especially valuable, so budgets follow the work rather than the map.
How does the time difference work for campaign management?+
It does not touch you. Monitoring, changes and reporting all run to Perth hours, and your account manager is reachable in your business day. The distance is our logistics problem, and the flat $300 a week ex GST management rate carries no WA loading.
How much ad spend does a Perth business need?+
Less than the east coast for the same result, usually. Most Perth service businesses get real traction from $1,000 to $2,000 a month thanks to cheaper audiences, and the audit sizes your actual market before recommending a number.
How fast do Perth Meta campaigns produce leads?+
Typically week one or two, faster than east-coast equivalents because cheaper audiences mean the pixel gathers learning data quicker. The compounding gains land over the first six to eight weeks as testing locks in your winning creative.
What creative should a Perth business use?+
Yours. Real jobs, your actual team, recognisable Perth backdrops, built in-house into feed-native ads. WA audiences can spot east-coast stock content instantly, and they scroll straight past it. Local proof is the whole game here.
Why do Perth businesses move their Meta accounts to Nexevo?+
The pattern is consistent: an agency two timezones away, reports full of impressions, creative recycled from national templates. We fix the clock, the tracking and the creative, and month-to-month terms mean the results have to keep earning the retainer.
