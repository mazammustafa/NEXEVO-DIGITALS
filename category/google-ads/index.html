A Google Ads agency built for American service businesses

Running Google Ads for a plumber in Staten Island is a different job to running them for a national ecommerce brand, and most agencies manage both the same way. We don't. Our campaigns are built around how service businesses actually win: local intent keywords, call and form tracking that ties back to booked jobs, ad schedules that match when your phones are manned, and budgets weighted to the neighborhoods and services that make you the most money. Whether that's emergency call-outs on Search, a Shopping feed for your supply arm, or Performance Max mopping up demand across YouTube, Gmail and Maps, every piece reports to one number: what a booked job costs you.

And because the landing pages, tracking and campaigns are all built under one roof by the same New York team, there's no gap between the click and the conversion for problems to hide in. That's the boring, structural reason our accounts outperform, and it's also why we're comfortable showing you everything: your account, your data, your results, in reporting you can read over a coffee.

Ask us anything
How much does Google Ads management cost?+
Our management fee depends on your ad spend and how many campaign types you're running, and every level is listed openly on our Google Ads packages page. Month to month, no lock-in contracts, and the fee never eats your media budget by stealth.
What's the minimum budget to make Google Ads work?+
It depends on your market and your cost per click, and we'll tell you straight in the audit. For most American service businesses, meaningful results start somewhere between $1,500 and $3,000 a month in ad spend. If your budget can't win in your market, we'd rather tell you that than take a fee.
Do I own my Google Ads account?+
Always. The account, the data and the history are yours, we just manage them. If you ever leave, everything stays with you, which is not how every agency plays it.
How fast will I see results?+
Paid search produces leads quickly, most accounts see movement in the first two to four weeks, and properly compounding results by month three as the data builds. We set honest expectations per channel before anything launches.
Can you fix an account another agency has been running?+
It's half of what we do. We audit what's there, keep what's genuinely working, rebuild what isn't, and show you exactly what was holding it back, usually structure, negatives and tracking.
What's the difference between Search and Performance Max?+
Search ads target the exact phrases people type, maximum control and intent. Performance Max spreads across all of Google's inventory and leans on machine learning, powerful with the right data feeding it, wasteful without. Most of our accounts run both, structured so they don't cannibalise each other.
Do you build the landing pages too?+
Yes, in-house, and honestly it's why our cost per lead beats agencies who only touch the ads. The click is half the job, the page it lands on is the other half.
Will I actually know what's going on?+
You'll get reporting you can read in two minutes: spend, leads, cost per lead, and what we're doing next. Plus direct access to the specialist running your account, not an account-manager buffer.
Why choose Nexevo Digital as your Google Ads agency?+
Because we're a Google Partner agency that treats your budget like our own money, answers within the hour, runs everything in-house in the US, and ties every report back to booked jobs, not clicks. And if we're not the right fit, the audit will tell you that for free.
