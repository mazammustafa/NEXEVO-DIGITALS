Ask us anything
What's included in a Meta Ads package?+
Campaign strategy, audience research, ad creative direction and copywriting, Meta Pixel setup, conversion tracking and monthly reporting, run by an experienced account manager you can contact directly. The landing page tier adds a purpose-built page and A/B creative testing.
Does the package include my ad spend?+
No, and no honest package does. The management fee covers the work; your ad spend goes directly to Meta and stays fully in your control, adjustable any time as goals and seasons change.
How much should I budget for Meta ad spend?+
Most businesses see solid early traction between $1,000 and $3,000 a month, with competitive industries or broad audiences benefiting from more. The real requirement is enough budget for the Pixel to gather data and the algorithm to find your converters, we'll give you a straight number for your market in the audit.
How fast will Meta Ads generate leads?+
Traffic and inquiries often start within the first week. The real gains land across the first four to eight weeks as the Pixel builds data, audiences sharpen and we find which creative produces the cheapest leads.
Facebook or Instagram, which do I need?+
Both, usually. Facebook tends to win for service-business lead generation, Instagram for visual brands and e-commerce, and a properly structured Meta campaign tests both placements and sends the budget wherever your audience actually converts.
Can I upgrade or change packages later?+
Any time. Accounts commonly start on Management, prove the channel, then add the landing page or bigger budgets as the numbers justify it. Month to month means nothing is locked.
