Quick answers to what people usually ask before their first call.

How quickly will you reply?+
Usually within the hour during business hours, Monday to Friday. Messages sent after hours are answered first thing the next morning.
Is the strategy call really free?+
Yes. It's a no-obligation conversation about your goals and your numbers. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you straight.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+
No lock-in contracts, ever. Everything is month to month, so we have to keep earning your business.
What should I have ready for the call?+
Nothing formal. A rough idea of your goals, budget and any accounts you already run is plenty. We'll do the digging.
