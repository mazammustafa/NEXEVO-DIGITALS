From $40K months to $180K months: the Melbourne Gas & Air transformation

There are plenty of ways to dress up marketing results, but for a business owner only one number really counts: what the month did. When Melbourne Gas & Air came onboard, their months were running around $40,000. Today they run around $180,000, and the word that matters most in that sentence is the one that comes next: consistently.

Not a spike. Not one good month propped up by a lucky job. A new baseline, four and a half times the old one, produced by a marketing system rather than a marketing campaign.

What a full takeover actually means

Melbourne Gas & Air handed us the whole engine: the website, the SEO, the Google Ads and the Meta Ads, all built and run by one team with one scoreboard. That matters more than it sounds, because a heating and cooling business in Melbourne is competing in the hardest advertising market in the country. Clicks are expensive, buyers compare quotes hard, and every agency-built account in the city is fighting over the same searches. Winning here is not about spending more. It is about every channel pulling in the same direction.

From $40,000 months to $180,000 months. Not a good month. The new normal.

How the system compounds

The website was rebuilt first, because it is the conversion layer every other dollar flows through. Fast, clear, built to turn a comparison shopper into a booked quote. Then the channels went to work on top of it, each one feeding the others.

Google Ads captured the demand that already existed, people searching for exactly what Melbourne Gas & Air does, with the account structured suburb by suburb around where the profitable work comes from. The search term data from those campaigns told the SEO exactly which pages and keywords to build, so the paid wins kept converting into organic rankings that do not send an invoice. Meta Ads worked the other end, putting the business in front of people before they searched, and retargeting the ones who visited but were not ready yet. In a trade where demand swings with the weather, having all four channels under one roof means the budget follows the season instead of fighting it.

That is why the growth held instead of spiking. Campaigns produce months. Assets produce baselines. Every month of this system added assets: rankings that compound, pixel data that sharpens the targeting, reviews that lift every channel at once, and a website that converts a little better than it did the month before.

The results, in one place

  • Monthly revenue up from around $40,000 to around $180,000, held consistently
  • A four and a half times lift in the number that actually matters to an owner
  • Full takeover: website, SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads, one team, one scoreboard
  • Built for Melbourne, the most competitive advertising market in Australia
  • Every channel tracked through to booked work, so the growth is provable, not vibes

Could your business do this?

Melbourne Gas & Air’s numbers are theirs, and any agency promising you a carbon copy is selling you something. What transfers is the structure: one team running every channel, a website built to convert, and honest tracking from click to booked job. If your months have been flat while your ad spend has not, the free growth session is where we look at your numbers and tell you, straight, what the full system could realistically do for you.
