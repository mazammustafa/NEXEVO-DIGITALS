Ask us anything
How long does SEO take to work in New York?+
Longer than a country town, faster than people fear. Most New York businesses see real movement inside three to six months, with map pack wins often arriving earlier. The honest variable is your niche's competition, which is exactly what the free audit measures before we talk timelines.
Can a small business actually rank against New York's big players?+
Yes, by refusing to fight the whole city at once. We win neighborhood clusters and service niches first, where focus beats size, then expand from strongholds. Chasing one giant citywide keyword from day one is how SEO budgets die.
Which New York neighborhoods can you rank us in?+
Wherever your work actually comes from: Queens, Westchester, the Upper West Side, the whole metro if that is your service area. Neighborhood relevance is earned page by page, so we prioritize by job value and expand as wins stack.
Do you guarantee first-page rankings?+
No, and you should run from anyone who does. Google sells ads, not guarantees, and no agency controls the algorithm. What we guarantee is the work: itemized monthly, tied to rankings and inquiries, on a month-to-month agreement that only survives if the results do.
What does SEO cost in New York?+
Our packages run $500 to $1,500 a week, month to month, every deliverable itemized. New York's competitiveness usually rewards the middle and upper tiers, but the audit will tell you honestly what your niche requires rather than what we would enjoy selling.
Is the map pack or organic more important for New York businesses?+
For local services, the map pack usually converts harder because it shows first with reviews and a call button. But the two feed each other, and page-one organic underneath the pack is what makes you the obvious choice. We work both battlegrounds at once.
Can you fix a Google penalty or a rankings drop?+
Usually, once we diagnose it. Drops tend to trace to technical issues, thin content, dodgy old links or an algorithm update shifting the goalposts. The audit finds the cause first, because prescribing before diagnosing is how bad agencies burn your money.
Why choose a New York SEO agency over a cheap offshore one?+
Because New York search is local knowledge all the way down: neighborhood behavior, seasonal trade patterns, how locals phrase the job. We rank our own business in this market and can show you live proof, then apply the same system to yours.
