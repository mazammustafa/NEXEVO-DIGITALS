Ask us anything
What do Meta Ads cost in New York?+
Management is a flat $300 a week on our packages, and the ad spend is yours, paid straight to Meta. New York audience costs run above the national average, so most local service businesses see solid traction from $1,500 to $3,000 a month in spend, sized properly in the audit.
Can you target just my part of New York?+
Precisely, and you should. We build radius and neighborhood targeting around your real service area and weight budget to the postcodes that produce booked work. Citywide targeting is the fastest way to donate a New York budget to Meta.
What creative works on New York audiences?+
Real over slick. Genuine jobs, recognizable streets, actual team members, phone-shot energy. New York feeds are saturated with polish, so authentic local content earns the stop, and we produce it in-house so it never goes stale.
How fast do Meta Ads produce leads in New York?+
Inquiries usually start inside the first week. The compounding gains land across the first four to eight weeks as the pixel learns and testing finds your angles. Every lead is tracked to its ad and neighborhood, so the winners get the budget.
Do Meta Ads work for trades and service businesses, or just online shops?+
They fill trade calendars all over New York when run properly: conversion objectives, lead forms or landing pages that make inquiring effortless, and retargeting for the comparers. The Boost button failing for you was not Meta failing. It was the wrong machine.
What is a realistic cost per lead in New York?+
It swings by industry, from single digits for broad home services to much higher for big-ticket work. What matters is the relationship to job value, which the audit models for your actual business before a dollar is spent.
Do we need new content filmed for the ads?+
Your job photos and phone footage are usually the best starting material, and we build the creative from them in-house. Where gaps exist we help you capture what is missing. Stock photography is the last resort, because New York scrolls straight past it.
Meta or Google Ads for a New York business?+
Different jobs. Google captures people already searching; Meta creates and captures demand before the search, then retargets the undecided. The strongest New York accounts we run pair them, and if the budget only allows one, the audit tells you honestly which fits your market first.
