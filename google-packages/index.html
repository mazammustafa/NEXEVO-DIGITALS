Ask us anything
What's included in a Google Ads package?+
Campaign setup or rebuild, keyword research, ad creation, bid management, weekly optimisation and monthly reporting, run by an experienced account manager you can contact directly. The higher tiers add a purpose-built landing page and bulk suburb campaign builds for multi-area businesses.
Does the package include my ad spend?+
No, and no honest package does. The management fee covers the work; your ad spend goes directly to Google and stays fully in your control. We'll recommend a budget for your industry and goals in the audit, and you can adjust it any time.
How much should I budget for ad spend?+
Enough to compete in your market and generate data worth learning from, which varies a lot by industry and city. Most local service businesses land somewhere between a few hundred and a couple of thousand dollars a week. We'll give you a straight number for your situation before you commit to anything.
Can I upgrade or change packages later?+
Any time. Businesses grow, expand into new services and suburbs, and packages flex with that: extra campaigns, another landing page, a bulk build when you're ready to blanket a region. Month to month means nothing is locked.
How fast will I see leads?+
Campaigns start driving traffic the day they launch, and most clients see consistent lead flow within four to six weeks as targeting and ads get tuned against real search data. Accounts with the landing page tier typically get there faster, because the click and the conversion are built together.
Why is there no lock-in contract?+
Because lock-ins protect agencies, not clients. We'd rather keep your business by performing than by contract, so every package runs month to month and the account, the data and everything we build stays yours. The pressure to deliver sits exactly where it should: on us.
